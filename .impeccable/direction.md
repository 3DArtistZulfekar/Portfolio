# Portfolio surface

Mode: Experience. User approved dark cinematic work, large type and electric accents, and building directly in Three.js.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A digital sculpture exhibition, with a real helmet suspended across monumental lettering. The work owns the screen, followed by generous editorial plates.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian backdrop, bone-white condensed display, acid-yellow fields and fine gray rules. Manrope for readable prose. Narrow squared controls, exceptionally large titles, and deliberately varied photographic crops.

STORY: Enter the sculpture, orbit it, switch to its underlying mesh, explore actual projects and models, then contact the artist. A separate studio makes adding and previewing models easy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim navigation across the top. The enormous two-line heading occupies the width, with the helmet crossing in front of the second line; three concise side annotations, fine floor rings, live material controls and a bottom-edge work link complete the scene.

FORM: User-pinned cinematic sculpture gallery. Seed 56fae3f5, assigned grounded candidate 3. Other grounded material: projection room, film contact sheet, sculpture plinth (chosen), foundry, exhibition catalog, kinetic mobile, material archive. Pinned direction beats catalog challengers; keep their discipline of purposeful state, structural repetition, and spatial honesty, without borrowing visual motifs.

SIGNATURE: A floating, lit actual FBX sculpture with damped orbit controls, pointer response, and a live wireframe reveal. Visible controls and reduced-motion support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
