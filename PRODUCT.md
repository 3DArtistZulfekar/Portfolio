## Users

Visitors evaluating Zulfekar Ahmad's 3D art, and the artist adding and presenting models.

## Product Purpose

Showcase real-time 3D models, environments, product visualization, and production configurator work, with direct contact and an easy model-and-texture upload workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing static HTML/JavaScript site and GitHub Pages deployment. Models and project metadata already exist in assets/models and data. Publishing requires the artist's GitHub write credential; local drafts must be identified as local. Support GLB, glTF, FBX, and OBJ, with companion textures and material files where needed.

## Brand Commitments

Keep Zulfekar Ahmad's existing identity and factual project content. The user approved a dark cinematic 3D gallery, oversized typography, electric accents, and delegated creative direction. The requested reference is the craft and ambition of Awwwards showcase websites.

## Evidence on Hand

data/personal.json, data/models.json, assets/models, data/personal_photo.png, and the existing resume and external portfolio links. Do not invent client relationships, awards, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the artist's work and real interactive 3D.
- Make adding models straightforward and previewable.
- Clearly distinguish saved drafts from public publishing.
- Keep the portfolio usable on phones, with reduced motion, and without WebGL.
